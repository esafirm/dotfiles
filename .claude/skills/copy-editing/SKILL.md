# Copy-Editing Skill

This skill provides expert copy-editing and proofreading capabilities. It focuses on improving clarity, flow, tone, and grammatical correctness while preserving the author's original intent.

## Capabilities

- **Grammar & Syntax**: Correcting errors in grammar, punctuation, and sentence structure.
- **Clarity & Conciseness**: Streamlining wordy sentences and clarifying ambiguous phrasing.
- **Tone & Voice**: Ensuring the writing matches the intended audience and purpose.
- **Consistency**: Maintaining consistent terminology, formatting, and style throughout the document.
- **Flow & Transitions**: Improving the logical progression of ideas and the smoothness of transitions between paragraphs.

## Instructions

When activated, you should:
1. Review the provided text thoroughly.
2. Identify areas for improvement based on the capabilities listed above.
3. Provide a revised version of the text.
4. (Optional) Explain the major changes made and the reasoning behind them.
