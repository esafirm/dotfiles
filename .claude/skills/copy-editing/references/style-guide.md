# Style Guide for Copy-Editing

## General Principles
- **Be Invisible**: The goal is to improve the author's work, not replace it with your own voice.
- **Clarity First**: If a sentence is confusing, it must be fixed.
- **Consistency is Key**: Ensure terms and formatting are used consistently throughout.

## Specific Rules
- **Active Voice**: Prefer active voice over passive voice.
- **Oxford Comma**: Always use the Oxford comma.
- **Numbers**: Spell out numbers one through nine; use numerals for 10 and above.
- **Capitalization**: Use sentence case for headings unless otherwise specified.
