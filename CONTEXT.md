# Android Startup Investigation

The language used by the personal Android startup investigation skill to describe comparable launch scenarios and outcomes.

## Language

**Stopped-app launch**:
A launch of an installed app whose process is not running, with its existing app data preserved. It is distinct from a data-cleared launch or a first launch after installation.
_Avoid_: Cold start

**Startup comparison**:
A baseline and a candidate measured under the same device, app variant, and launch scenario. Their observed difference is not itself proof of the cause of that difference.
